Computer Architecture · ML Systems

Yun Seok Kim

I study hardware-software co-design for emerging machine learning applications and efficient AI systems at scale.

I am a final-year master’s student at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Won Woo Ro in Embedded Systems and Computer Architecture Lab (eSCaL).

My research spans efficient generative models and memory-centric architectures. I am interested in how workload characteristics can guide algorithm, data mapping, and hardware design.

ML Accelerators & Co-design

Memory-Centric ML Systems

Processing-in-memory architectures, topology-aware model parallelism, and workload mapping for efficient attention execution.

Memory-Centric Architecture Using Reconfigurable PIM Devices

Analyzing FlashAttention execution on reconfigurable processing-in-memory architectures, with a focus on workload mapping, data allocation, and coordination among hardware components.
